As a reminder per your Optum agreement , Article 2.1, Provision of MHSA Services, providers are required to comply with all terms and conditions of the Provider Manual, protocols, and the agreement, and to perform duties as a participating provider within the UBH network as designated.

 

Additionally, please note that the public Provider Manual outlines key requirements and enforcement standards. On page 56, it specifies Treatment Record Content Standards, including documentation of the date of service, start and stop time or total session time, session attendees, diagnosis, services rendered, and the rendering clinician’s name, credentials, license, and applicable identification number. Further, pages 74–75 state that in the event potential fraud, waste, and/or abuse is identified, appropriate corrective actions may be implemented using a range of tools, from provider education to full recoupment of improperly paid funds, and that Optum works closely with state and federal agencies to combat fraud, waste, and abuse, referring suspected or confirmed cases as required by regulation and contract.

 

For further reference, please review the Fraud, Waste, Abuse, Error, and Payment Integrity resource page, which provides additional guidance.
